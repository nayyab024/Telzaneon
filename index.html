Every call,
qualified automatically.

An AI calling agent that holds natural, human-like conversations at scale. It answers, qualifies, handles objections and transfers only the best prospects to your team.

  • AI voice agent
  • Real-time qualification
  • Live transfer

It dials.
It says hello.Every call,
answered first ring.

The agent dials your leads automatically, introduces itself and listens. Answering machines and busy signals are detected and disposed instantly.Day, night or weekend, the agent picks up instantly, greets callers in your business’s name and listens.

A pitch that
sounds local.It understands
what they need.

A state-specific, personalised pitch based on where the lead is calling from, in the voice persona that converts best.Prices, hours, availability or an existing order: answered from your own business knowledge, in a natural voice.

Qualified
in real time.Qualified
while they talk.

Qualifying questions are validated against each campaign’s criteria: age range, location, intent and do-not-call status.The agent captures the caller’s details and checks them against your rules: service area, property, timing and fit.

Objections?
Handled.Edge cases,
covered.

“Not interested”, “call me back” or “who is this?” Natural rebuttals keep the conversation going without breaking the flow.“Can I speak to a person?” “Call me tomorrow.” Requests are handled politely, with callbacks booked automatically.

Only the best
reach your agents.Transferred, booked
or routed.

Qualified callers are transferred live with zero dead air. Every call ends with a precise disposition, synced back to your dialer or CRM.Ready callers go live to your team with a summary. Others get an appointment or a callback. Every call is logged.

A voice for every state, client or test

  • Per-state personalisation. A pitch recorded for the caller’s state, so every conversation feels local.
  • Multiple voice personas. Swap voices per client or campaign, or A/B test them side by side.
  • Natural pacing. Natural pauses, regional inflection and a conversational tone.

A reporting portal for every client

  • Live dashboard of calls and transfers
  • Call-by-call records
  • Transfer rate by day, campaign, state and voice
  • Lead quality scores by source
  • Recording access

All reporting is scoped per client. Each account sees only its own data.

Compliant by default, not by add-on

  • Transfer numbers and campaign settings managed per client, with access controls

What does Telza do?+

Telza builds AI agents that move the work forward: the Voice Agent for inbound and outbound calls, Fillo AI for lead-ID form automation, the WhatsApp Bot, E-commerce AI and custom workflows. We also build the portals, CRMs, web and mobile apps around them.

Can the Voice Agent handle inbound and outbound calls?+

Yes. Outbound, it dials your leads, pitches, qualifies, handles objections and transfers qualified callers live. Inbound, it answers every call on the first ring, answers questions, qualifies and transfers, books or routes the caller.

Which dialers and carriers does it work with?+

ViciDial natively (agent status, disposition and call ID sync), plus Telnyx, Twilio, Vonage, SignalWire and any SIP-compatible carrier through a standard trunk configuration.

Do I need all the products?+

No. Start with the task you want to improve. We help identify the right product or scope a custom workflow around your needs.

Do you build portals, CRMs and apps too?+

Yes. Customer portals, custom CRMs, web applications, iOS and Android apps, websites and Shopify stores, connected to your AI agents and existing tools.

What happens in a demo?+

We discuss the work you want to automate, walk through a relevant live example and outline a possible implementation. Book a meeting, or try the live demo first.

The call journey

What happens when a customer calls

  1. 01

    The phone rings

    Telza answers the incoming call straight away, around the clock.

  2. 02

    The caller is heard

    Customers explain what they need in their own words, and Telza responds to what they actually say.

  3. 03

    Details are collected

    Telza asks for the information your process needs and captures it during the call.

  4. 04

    The request is handled

    Supported inquiries are answered and configured workflows are completed while the caller is still on the line.

  5. 05

    Resolved, or handed to your team

    When a person should take over, Telza transfers the call to your team.

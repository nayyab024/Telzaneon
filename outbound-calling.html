The call journey

What happens when Telza makes the call

  1. 01

    The campaign starts

    Your contact list, goal and script load, and calls only go out inside the calling windows you set.

  2. 02

    Telza dials

    The call goes out. If voicemail answers, Telza leaves a tailored message and schedules a retry.

  3. 03

    A personal opening

    Telza greets the lead by name and picks up from what they asked for, using details from your CRM.

  4. 04

    Qualify and handle objections

    Your qualifying questions are asked and common objections are answered, following your campaign logic.

  5. 05

    Booked, logged or handed over

    The appointment is booked or the conversation is routed to your team, and the outcome is written to your CRM.
